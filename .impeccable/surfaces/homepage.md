# Homepage streaming experience

- Route: `src/app/page.tsx`
- Mode: Operate — help learners discover and start a photography course.
- Audience: people learning studio and commercial photography.
- Priority: cinematic featured course, then horizontal course discovery rows; keep membership and instructor information below the catalog.
- Approved composition: `.impeccable/mocks/homepage-comp-1.png`
- Required interactions: play and details links, a React-state “Mi Lista” toggle, horizontally scrollable course rows with hover/focus arrows, and card-level play/details actions on hover/focus.
- Content constraints: use existing course titles and imagery; preserve configured membership plans; do not show unverified ratings, audience counts, statistics, or testimonials.
- Visual constraints: preserve Glewstudio's identity and Manrope; use OLED `#0A0A0F`, restrained `#E8B86D`, dark gradients and translucent action controls.
